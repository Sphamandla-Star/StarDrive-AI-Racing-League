# Tuning Card — `drive_example()` in `snakeoil3_gym.py`

> **Read-only reference.** Edit the values in `snakeoil3_gym.py`; do not edit this file during a run.

| Setting | Line | Current Value | What It Controls | Raise it → | Lower it → | Crash Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `target_speed` | 533 | `300` | The top speed (km/h) the car tries to reach on a straight | Car chases a higher top speed, accelerates longer | Car lifts off the throttle earlier, slower overall pace | **High** — 300 is already very aggressive; raising it further leaves almost no braking room |
| Steer-to-angle gain (`*15`) | 536 | `15` | How hard the car steers to align itself with the track direction | Snappier alignment corrections, more responsive | Lazier alignment, car drifts further before correcting | **Medium** — too high causes oscillation / tank-slapping |
| Steer-to-centre gain (`*.10`) | 538 | `0.10` | How aggressively the car steers back to the lane centre | Stronger centre-hugging, tighter line | Car wanders wider before correcting | **Low** — affects lane position more than speed |
| Throttle ramp-up step (`+= .01`) | 542 | `0.01` | How quickly the throttle opens each simulation step when below target speed | Faster acceleration response | Sluggish throttle build-up | **Low** — tiny per-step change; mainly affects acceleration feel |
| Throttle ramp-down step (`-= .01`) | 544 | `0.01` | How quickly the throttle closes each step when at or above target speed | Car holds high throttle longer before backing off | Car lifts off throttle more aggressively | **Medium** — asymmetry with ramp-up can cause speed hunting |
| Low-speed throttle boost (`1/(speedX+.1)`) | 546 | `1` (coefficient) | Extra throttle injected when `speedX < 10` to prevent stalling | Bigger launch kick, quicker getaway from rest | Weaker launch, more likely to stall or crawl | **Low** — only active at very low speeds |
| Steer-speed coupling (`*50`) | 541 | `50` | Reduces the target speed proportionally while steering (corner slowdown) | Stronger slowdown in corners — more conservative cornering | Less slowdown — car carries more speed through corners | **High** — lowering this means the car brakes less for bends |
| Traction-control threshold (`> 5`) | 550 | `5` rad/s | Rear-vs-front wheel spin difference that triggers throttle cut | TC kicks in less often — more wheelspin allowed | TC fires at smaller differences — very sensitive, kills traction early | **Medium** — too high = sustained oversteer; too low = constant throttle chopping |
| Traction-control cut amount (`-= .2`) | 551 | `0.2` | How much throttle is removed per step when TC triggers | Bigger chop — aggressive traction recovery | Smaller chop — gentler correction, may not stop the spin | **Medium** — too small and the rear keeps stepping out |
| Gear 2 threshold | 555 | `50` km/h | Speed at which the car upshifts from 1st to 2nd | Holds 1st gear longer, higher revs before shifting | Upshifts to 2nd very early, low-rev 1st gear | **Low** — gear timing affects speed, not directly crash risk |
| Gear 3 threshold | 557 | `80` km/h | Speed at which the car upshifts from 2nd to 3rd | Longer time in 2nd | Shifts to 3rd sooner | **Low** |
| Gear 4 threshold | 559 | `110` km/h | Speed at which the car upshifts from 3rd to 4th | Longer time in 3rd | Shifts to 4th sooner | **Low** |
| Gear 5 threshold | 561 | `140` km/h | Speed at which the car upshifts from 4th to 5th | Longer time in 4th | Shifts to 5th sooner | **Low** |
| Gear 6 threshold | 563 | `170` km/h | Speed at which the car upshifts from 5th to 6th | Longer time in 5th, higher revs | Shifts to 6th sooner, lower revs at top speed | **Low** |

### Quick-reference: safe starting experiments

1. **Slow the car down safely** → lower `target_speed` to `150` and raise the steer-speed coupling to `80`.
2. **Tighten cornering** → raise steer-to-angle gain to `18` and steer-to-centre gain to `0.15`.
3. **Reduce wheelspin on launch** → lower the TC threshold from `5` to `3`.
4. **Smoother speed control** → increase both throttle ramp steps from `0.01` to `0.02`.
